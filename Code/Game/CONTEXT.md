# Game
Library documentation for `Code/Game`. Read `.claude/CLAUDE.md` first (rules, building, style, dependency direction). The game components (`GameUnitComponent`/`GameStructureComponent`/`GameProjectileComponent`) live in Entity — see `Code/Entity/CONTEXT.md`; unit steering over the flow fields is in `Code/Nav/CONTEXT.md`.

* CO-OP PVE (`--coop`, requires `--game`; CLIENTS pass both too — the world layout is built locally, only the sim mirrors): its own GENERATED WORLD — no corridor, a big square (`c_coopHalfSize` 180 m half-size on the 400 m ground plane) of RANDOM IMPASSABLE TERRAIN. `GameMatch::rebuildCoopMap(seed, fill, lanes)`: 10 m cells (`c_coopCells` 36², aligned 5:1 with the 2 m build grid; the SAME `CoopMap` grid + `finishGrid`/`spawnTerrain`/`clampToOpenGround` machinery builds the PvP arenas at 5 m cells — see the PvP paragraph), two-octave value noise thresholded to EXACTLY "Terrain fill" (sorted-copy threshold), a rock-free Base zone (`c_coopBaseClearRadius` 26), "Terrain lanes" carved wobbling attack lanes from the Base ring out past the edge, then a 4-connected BFS FLOOD FILL from the Base that turns every unreachable open pocket INTO rock — so all open ground (nodes, ambient spawns, move orders) is reachable by construction, and `CoopMap::depth` = geodesic BFS distance. Blocked cells spawn `rock.pre` (10 m cube, sunk to a 3.5–6 m exposed height) under ONE `CoopTerrain` root via spawnBatch; horizontal runs merge into `m_terrainRects` = the Nav obstacles AND `StructureSystem::setTerrainBlocked` rects (cellsFree refuses them — red ghosts, no building in rock, unit-spawn probes skip). EDGE BARRIER at ±180: `barrier.pre` ring (20 m segments, E/W yaw'd 90°) whose collider is `Layer Barrier, CollidesWith Player` — blocks ONLY player capsules (player.pre now carries `Layer Player`, mask All); units/shots walk through. Visuals: post per segment + `drawCoopBarrier`'s pulsing energy lines (3 heights, real clock). Placement bounds = inside the barrier. DETERMINISM + SYNC: generation is pure seeded math (`MapRng`/`mapHash` — NEVER glm::linearRand) so every instance derives the identical map; the AUTHORITY rolls the seed ("Game/Coop/Map seed", 0 = random) and broadcasts seed+fill+lanes as the "GMp" event — FIRST in `onClientJoined` (before the GPl replay; reliable ch1 order) — clients defer terrain+nodes until it arrives (`rebuildCoopMap` no-ops on repeats); fill/lanes ride GMp (and the F9 save as MapSeed/MapFill/MapLanes — loadGame regenerates the exact map, clearing structures first) because a joiner's tweak sync lands after the replay. Nodes: a STARTER mineral+fuel pair ~10 m from the Base, then golden-angle-spiral candidates SNAPPED to reachable open cells with a 13 m spacing floor. A move order/route waypoint clicked into rock clamps to the nearest open cell (`clampToOpenGround`). Every player on team 0 around ONE central Base (`allocateClientTeam` returns 0, `teamStartPos` is the shared start); the AI team `GameMatch::CoopAiTeam` (= 1, NOT a high slot) fields UNITS ONLY, no structures. Co-op runs the Force system at TWO LIVE TEAMS (`ForceSystem::setNumTeams(2)` in the GameMatch ctor): every force shader recompiles with NUM_FORCE_TEAMS=2 and the bake volume/buffers shrink to fit (one RGBA16F volume, one vec4/sample readback) — so every co-op team index must stay < 2. SWARM unit (`ENpcType::Swarm`, `Entities/Game/swarmUnit.pre`): the cheap horde body — health only, NO Component Force (no bubble/battery/GPU readbacks) — built for many-thousand counts. Shield-less units interact with emitters through the BAKED PRESSURE FIELD (`ForceSystem::sampleBakedField(pos, team)` in the entity pass — a CPU bilinear tap, no per-unit GPU slot, unlimited unit counts): `opposing`/`opposingGradient` reproduce the SHIELDED units' exact push chain — -grad × 0.35 (the 13-sample integral's mean self-weight) × forceGain × pushGain × pressure × tension, speed-clamped — so ONE "Field push gain" tweak rules both paths; exposure damage is GRADED by field depth — `smoothstep(iso x "Field damage starts (x iso)", iso, opposing) x fieldDps x mult` — because the push equilibrium parks pressing units AT the surface (opposing ~ iso), where a binary inside test read false and units ground on shells unharmed; ~3 frames latent. FALLBACK when the bake is disabled ("Force/Bake/Enabled" off): `GameStructureComponent::bubbleRadius` (stamped next to `strainable` in the death sweep = reach × 0.5 × outputFrac) gives the same exposure damage CPU-side, no push. They still deposit `addLoad` strain; a shield-less body spawns with a ZERO battery (`energy = energyMax = 0` when shieldOutput ≤ 0 — GameUnitComponent::spawn), so the label pass's shield-vs-health branch and damage absorb never mistake it for shielded, and DAMAGED swarm show health bars like any unit (full bars hide, so a horde only labels its damaged slice). AMBIENT: "Ambient budget" POINTS of units scattered around the map at world start in small GROUPS (`AmbientSpawn`: 3–9 bodies of ONE archetype in a 4–9 m disc around an anchor on a random REACHABLE map cell — uniform by area, outside "Ambient safe radius"; `ambientPointNear` rejection-samples open ground and slides bodies 1.2 m off rock edges, so nothing lines up on the 10 m lattice) — same per-type "Cost *" tweaks as waves, and a group's archetype roll is gated by GEODESIC spawn depth (`CoopMap::depth`/maxDepth) the way waves gate by index (near ring = swarm-grade recipes, deep map = the full table incl. brute walls: fewer, tougher bodies out deep). Blocked cells also spawn `terrainmark.pre` (a 13 m dark-red ground plane, render-only) so the impassable footprint outlines every rock. EVERY unit's Nav team-field targeting is RANGE-GATED by "Target search radius" (geodesic Sample.dist — the field rebuilds ~0.25 s with LIVE source positions, so in-range units track a moving player tightly; out of range they hold their patch instead of marching across the map — this replaced the old per-unit `ambient` flag). Out of the search radius a non-locked `hasTarget` from an earlier tick is DROPPED at once (it is persistent component state; kept, the unit marched to the last known spot for up to a retarget interval and looked as if it ignored the follow radius), then the "Nav follow radius" band may still walk an existing crowd lane. Units splat "Game/Enemies/Steer/Flow splat gain" (0.5) x their measured velocity into that lane per tick. Wave units stay order-driven until arrival, then the same gated AI takes over. WAVES (They-are-Billions style): `tickWaves` arms the clock, `queueWave` picks a RANDOM compass direction projected onto the barrier square and stepped OUTSIDE it (the swarm spawns in the open ring between barrier and ground edge and walks in THROUGH the player-only barrier; cluster points that drift inside push back out along the dominant axis, clamped to `c_coopGroundEdge` 196), sizes the wave in BUDGET POINTS ("Wave budget" + "Wave budget growth"/wave; each type spends its "Cost <type>" tweak — swarm 1, brute 10 — so heavy archetypes field fewer bodies; the "Max enemy units" cap converts at the cheapest cost, and an unaffordable roll downgrades to the mix's cheapest type), seeds ONE lane and fires "GWv" (u16 index) at clients; `tickCoopSpawns` TRICKLES the actual spawns ("Spawns per frame" 24 — wave first, then ambient) so huge waves enter over seconds, not one hitch — and the trickle is THREADED: the frame's rolls (budget math + glm RNG, main-thread — linearRand is not thread-safe) collect into `NpcSystem::spawnLooseUnits`, one `World::spawnBatch` fan-out per frame with team/order/roster fixup serial after the join; composition = one ARCHETYPE per wave (`c_waveArchetypes` in Match.cpp: named recipes of ≤4 weighted types — pure rushes AND combined-arms mixes — gated by wave index so heavies unlock over time, never the same recipe twice in a row, weights jittered ±40%; `rollWaveType` samples the rolled mix per spawn; the wave log names it), each unit `orderMove`d to the Base's near face on the incoming side (lock releases on arrival, AI takes over). All spawns via `NpcSystem::spawnLooseUnit` (sourceId 0 — no route, no death accounting). Authority HUD shows "Next wave (s)". KNOWN gaps: the Base takes damage but the death sweep never destroys it (no lose condition yet); the wave clock/pending counts are not saved (F10 re-arms); thousands of dynamic bodies is the AIM, not yet a measured budget (knobs: Max enemy units, Spawns per frame, box3d "Physics/World/Worker count")
* The actual game built on the engine (`Code/Game`, static lib linked by App). Top-down tactical PVP whitebox (the OLD PvE — ambient field, player combat — is REMOVED; the co-op mode above is the new PvE): place Force emitter pylons + economy to siege the other team. `App.exe --game` = single player sandbox; MULTIPLAYER = `--game --server` (WINDOWED listen server — GPU readbacks drive the sim, so `--headless` refuses `--game`) + `--game --connect <ip>`. Server runs ALL sim; sync is three-layered: (1) player-structure MIRROR over game events — GPl/GRm reliable on change + join replay (idempotent mirror* appliers in StructureSystem, notified via its onStructure* hooks; a completed blueprint RE-SENDS GPl via `onStructureBuilt`, and `mirrorPlace` on a known id applies the built flag idempotently — cable segments sit outside GSt, so this IS their built notification; there is NO cable wire at all: links derive locally on both sides — see Economy) + GSt at 5 Hz (u8-quantized health/charge/fuel/minerals/outputFrac/flow-utilization/powered for the first 79 NON-CABLE structures + resource totals; mirrored emitters drive their LOCAL ForceComponent from the synced outputFrac, so client-side fields are real and the client player's shield readbacks work); (2) units/shots replicate as network entities (`Component Network` in those prefabs + player.pre); (3) each client ADOPTS its server-spawned capsule (`GamePlayer::clientAdopt`, LocalOwner scan) and owner-simulates through the claim stream, computing shield/health locally. GAME BLOB (replaced the old GSh/GqE shield-mirror events): every snapshot/claim record for an entity WITH a GameUnitComponent carries `NetRecFlag_Game` + 5 bytes (`packGameStateBlob`/`applyGameStateBlob` in NetworkManager.cpp: healthFrac/energyFrac u8, emitter output over a fixed 0..8 range, materialsFrac, flags = collapsed | team<<4). Server-side it is CHANGE-DETECTED (`ServerState::lastSentGameBlob`) and forces the record out even when the pose alone would skip (asleep body / unmoved entity) — a collapse edge flushes on the next snapshot tick. Receivers write the entity's GameUnitComponent + drive its LOCAL ForceComponent output/team, so remote bubbles render true drain/collapse state on the right team and the overhead labels read components directly on every instance; the entity's OWNER keeps its self-computed shield (applyShield off) and takes only server-authoritative materials; claims apply shield-only (never materials/team — client-forgeable). Player capsules are PUPPET GameUnitComponents (`Puppet true` in player.pre: update() is inert, GamePlayer stamps health/energy/collapsed/team each tick and reads materials back as LocalOwner; the server writes m_clientMaterials into the twins); puppets are excluded from turret targeting and NpcSystem clear/save. Client intents = Gq* request events (GqP place — cables included, GqD demolish, GqW routes), gated by a `setEventFilter` (only Gq* ≤64B from clients, installed by spawnWorld — during the LOBBY phase the LobbySystem's Lb*-only filter stands instead) and validated by the same authority seams as local input; the C++ dispatch hook `NetworkManager::setOnGameEvent` is owned by MAIN.CPP, installed ONCE and never replaced (it routes "Lb*" to the LobbySystem and everything else to `GameMatch::handleNetEvent`, now public) — GameMatch must NOT install its own hook: a lobby client's GameMatch is constructed INSIDE a dispatch, and reassigning the oc::function there would destroy the executing lambda. `GameMatch::onClientJoined/Left` (registered from main when game+server) spawn/remove per-client players + replay world state. Gameplay tweaks sync automatically (Core.Tweaks `Synced` flag — all Game/* except Game/Camera). TEAMS: everything is per-TEAM (`GameMaxTeams` 8, but the corridor map spawns one Base per PLAYABLE team, so `GameMatch::PlayableTeams` = 2 is the slot pool — a player without a Base has no respawn anchor, mineral bank or healing). The server = team 0; a joining client gets the LOWEST FREE slot (`allocateClientTeam`, extras double up on the last), stamped onto its capsule's puppet GameUnitComponent + emitter at spawn. That component IS the record: `clientTeam(clientId)` reads it back (−1 = no capsule → the Gq* handlers refuse the request), and the OWNER learns its own team from the snapshot game blob (`applyTeam` deliberately applies outside the `applyShield` gate, so an owner that computes its own shield still receives team + materials; claims never carry either — client-forgeable). NEVER derive a team from the clientId: ids are minted monotonically and never recycled, so a reconnect or a failed first attempt shifted the second connection onto a base-less team (the bug this replaced). GamePlayer follows the component on a client (gated on `hasTarget` so the prefab's authored team is never latched) and stamps it everywhere else. Per-team specifics: players'/projectiles' Force teams set at spawn/adopt (`GamePlayer::setTeam`), structures carry the builder's team (GPl + place requests carry a team byte; fields re-teamed after spawn since prefabs author team 0), demolish validates same-team, minerals/fuel are per-team arrays (GSt sends all 8; HUD/affordability read own team). The map is one of the PVP ARENAS (`EPvpMap`, the lobby's "Map" pick → `setPvpMap` before spawnWorld; the AUTHORITY builds it in spawnWorld via `rebuildPvpMap`, CLIENTS build whatever the server's GMp event names — GMp now carries a mode byte: 0 = the co-op inputs, 1 = the arena index — so a client has NO terrain or nodes until its join replay lands, and extractor node indices agree by construction): all ROCK TERRAIN on the co-op cell machinery (the `CoopMap` grid now carries its own cellsX/cellsZ/cellSize/origin; PvP uses 5 m cells = rock.pre/terrainmark.pre spawned at HALF scale, `c_pvpCellSize`) with a 10 m rock BORDER ring (`c_pvpBorderCells` 2 — the old borderwall.pre fence and `spawnCorridorWalls` are GONE; borderwall.pre is now unused) — `generatePvpGrid`: Lane = the original corridor (x ±65, z ±20), Wide lane (z ±40), Chokepoints (the wide lane + a 10 m middle wall at |x| < 5 pierced by three 10 m openings at z 0 / ±25), Circle (a 65 m open disc around a 28 m rock column, Bases mid-ring at radius 47 evenly around the circle, team 0 west — `baseGroundPos` is per arena; the lane arenas spread N Bases evenly along x between ±55); every Base cell gets a rock-free 8 m disc (a middle team on the Chokepoints wall line carves its own gap), then the shared `finishGrid` flood-fills from team 0's Base (sealing cut-off pockets) and merges the rects (Nav obstacles + `setTerrainBlocked` + placement bounds = the arena interior, `m_pvpInterior`). Nodes per arena (`spawnPvpNodes`, a node whose cell is rock is skipped): the lane's 180°-symmetric 8-node table, + an outer 6-node band on the wide arenas, the Circle 12 nodes on two rings between the Bases. A PvP F9 save carries `PvpMap`; loadGame regenerates a different arena (clearing structures first) like the co-op seed. Everything spawned deterministically on every instance with a Base at each end (team 0 at (−55,0,0), team 1 at (55,0,0); clients spawn/respawn beside theirs; both damageable but never destroyed); `setPlacementBounds` keeps footprints inside the walls. Structure damage: every placeable prefab has `ContactEvents true` — placeStructure installs an onContact hook (enemy-TEAM "Projectile" contacts chip "Projectile structure damage" health) — and tickDamage's rule: hostile = ANY other team's bubble owns the structure's query point (push your field over their buildings to siege). KNOWN gaps: player turrets target only units (not players); GSt caps at 79 structures. SAVE/LOAD (F9 save / F10 load, server/single-player only — clients refuse): full sim snapshot (structures with id/type/pos/facing/node/team/blueprint/stores/route — cable segments are ordinary Structure entries, NO Cable nodes; players/shots NOT saved) to `Assets/Local/gamesave.txt` via AssetParser (`StructureSystem::saveTo/loadFrom/clearAllStructures`, `NpcSystem::saveUnits/loadUnits`, `GameMatch::saveGame/loadGame`). * Economy: a node produces NOTHING until an extractor is built on it (one per node; the ghost snaps to the nearest free node within "Extractor snap radius", validated again at place time) AND that extractor is fed energy. ENERGY IS A FLOW NETWORK (tickPower): EVERY structure holds LOCAL charge (emitter/extractor an "Internal buffer" 10, generators a "Generator buffer" 20, battery "Battery capacity" 100; BARRACKS hold none — they run on conveyor-fed minerals alone, so no energy bar and no power cable attaches); each tick every derived link moves energy GRAVITY-FED toward consumers, capped by the MEDIUM's throughput ("Cable throughput" 5/s — tiers are gone, `m_cableThroughput[3]` by medium): three potential bands — producers (generator/solar) on top always exporting, battery storage in the middle BALANCING by fill fraction among themselves, consumers below outranking everything until FULL — cross-band links run at full throughput downhill (consumers fill first, surplus banks in batteries), same-band links move a DAMPED fraction (`c_equalizeDamping` 0.25) of the exact equalizing transfer — each source sizes its share without seeing the others, so undamped, N sources feeding one receiver overshoot the balance point together and bounce back next tick (wasted throughput + a flow direction that keeps reversing); damping stays stable up to 1/damping simultaneous sources and removed the need for any direction hysteresis in the draw. Energy still propagates hop by hop, thin lines starve, and a full generator buffer throttles production (export-limited = no fuel burn). Consumers drain their internal battery; empty = unpowered. FUEL IS A FLOW NETWORK TOO (SAME gravity-fed bands — extractor tanks export, fuel tanks balance, generator/fabricator burners fill first): powered fuel extractors fill their OWN small tank (full = production stalls), PIPELINE runs ("Pipeline throughput") move it, generators/fabricators burn from their OWN tank (fabricators need energy AND fuel). MINERALS ARE THE THIRD FLOW NETWORK (same band model, four levels: extractor/fabricator outputs (3) export into the Base (2), which exports into silos (1), which feed barracks/constructors (0) — the Base sits between producers and silos on purpose, so extractors still dump into it at full rate while its own trickle PREFERS flowing out over banking, keeping only what receivers cannot take; this replaced the old mineral-sink fiction): powered mineral extractors/fabricators fill their OWN buffer (full = stalls), CONVEYOR runs ("Conveyor throughput") move mineral stores, and only what sits in Mineral silos + the Base ("Mineral silo/base capacity" stores) is SPENDABLE (`m_minerals` per-team cache recomputed per tick; spending drains silos first, Base last). PHYSICAL CABLES (the link tools/point-to-point cables/Connector range links are all REMOVED): cables are 1-cell GRID STRUCTURES — `EStructureType::CablePower/CablePipe/CableConveyor` (one per medium, no tiers) + the 1x3 oriented `Crossing` bridge (a perpendicular cable passes UNDER its middle cell; it conducts whichever ONE medium its two END cells resolve to — each end accepts from THREE sides (outward + the two laterals; only the middle is pass-through-only), and a run on one end + a capacity-holding building on the other also counts). `GameStructureLink`s are DERIVED, never authored: `StructureSystem::rebuildDerivedLinks` (dirty-gated, main thread, in tickAuthority after the request drain and in tickMirror — CLIENTS derive locally from the mirrored segments, no cable wire) union-finds the BUILT segments over 4-neighbour equal-medium adjacency (blueprint segments break the path; the under-cable participates through `CellEntry::underId`, which is also what keeps a crossing from unioning with the cable below it), attaches every adjacent building with capacity > 0 in the run's medium (blueprint buildings pre-wire), BRIDGES through built buildings — a building conducts every medium it holds, so two same-medium runs touching it merge into one (blueprint buildings do not bridge; buildings NEVER connect by direct adjacency — a link always needs cable between them), then diffs desired-vs-live links: all attached pairs per run (clique up to `c_runCliqueCap` 16 buildings = 120 links; past that a STAR from a ROLE-picked hub — storage band 1 first (relays both directions, the old Connector's role), else a producer, lowest id tie-break: NEVER a plain consumer when better exists, since a band-0 hub cannot send energy UP to a battery — that starved batteries on big runs), owner = lower structureId so rebuilds never flip flow state. The CELL HASH (`m_cells`: cellKey → {id, underId}) is maintained at the spawn/remove seams (insertCells/eraseCells — a crossing demotes its under-cable to underId, removal promotes it back) and backs `cellsFree` (per-cell now: buildings refuse ANY occupied cell — no building on a cable; a cable may slot under a free crossing middle; a crossing may bridge exactly one cable; `ignoreCables` = the unit-spawn probe, since cables are walk-through), the derivation, and the arm visuals. SURFACE TENSION: pushing into bubbles scales push AND energy costs by (1 + tension × pressure) on BOTH sides ("Game/Shield/Surface tension", "Game/Enemies/Push tension", "Game/Structures/Pressure draw tension"); player/unit Accel defaults halved so steering loses the shoving match. CONSTRUCTION: placing is FREE — structures spawn as BLUEPRINTS (`Structure::blueprint`, gray tint via applyStructureTint, inert in every tick: no power/fields/income/flows, links pre-wire but carry nothing). HEALTH IS THE PROGRESS: a ghost spawns at 1 hp, materials HEAL it at cost/healthMax per hp (`investMaterials`), full health = built (blue health bar while blueprint; damage literally undoes construction). The SAME price repairs damaged built structures. CHEAT: "Game/Cheats/Free instant build" (Synced checkbox — the server's value rules all players) skips the blueprint phase — placements spawn built at full health, no materials. Materials arrive from (a) PLAYERS: a carried "Materials" inventory (HUD counter; "Game/Player/Materials max"; server-authoritative for ALL players — clients receive theirs via the snapshot game blob's materials byte) refilled near own-team Silos/Base/mineral-Extractors (`takeStoredMinerals` — hand-couriering a fresh dig site's buffer works before any conveyor exists) and invested into nearby BLUEPRINTS only (`fundNearbyBlueprint`, "Game/Construction" tweaks), dropped on death; or (b) the CONSTRUCTOR structure (Production hotbar, conveyor-fed mineral stock + powered, amber range ring in drawDebug) which funds the nearest own-team blueprint OR damaged structure in "Constructor range" (`fundNearbyBlueprint(..., includeRepairs)`); with NOTHING to build/repair it instead BOOSTS the nearest own-team barracks' spawn clock (`m_barracksBoost` map, +"Constructor boost rate" per constructor at "boost materials/s" + "boost energy/s"; Npc timers tick at 1+boost). Blueprint state mirrors via a GPl built flag + a GSt status-bits byte (bit0 powered, bit1 blueprint; caps GSt 79). NPC emitter drain (`addEmitterLoad`) is SHIELD-INDEPENDENT — collapsed units strain emitters like shielded ones. GRID PLACEMENT: every placement (extractors included — the node's position snaps too) aligns to a 2 m world grid (`StructureSystem::snapToGrid`; odd footprints center on a cell, even on a corner) and occupies `footprintExtent(type, rot)` cells (`footprintCellsOf`² for everything square: 1x1 default incl. cables; FuelTank/Bastion/Turret/Solar/Fabricator/MineralSilo 2x2; Barracks/Base 3x3; the Crossing is the ONE non-square footprint — 3x1/1x3 by its facing, quantized to ±X/±Z at placeStructure like the Lance and carried on GPl + the save's Facing, or client derivation diverges on which cells it covers; prefab Scale/HalfExtents = footprint half so boxes fill their cells. HEIGHTS vary via the baseshapes height variants — CubeHalf/CubeQuarter/Pillar2/Pillar3 are the 2 m cube with 0.5x/0.25x/2x/3x height, origin centered: Wall + Mineral silo = Pillar2, Battery = CubeHalf, Solar = CubeQuarter; cables are low Cylinder2 hubs (baseshapes' 2-high center-origin cylinder) with 4 render-only ARM child entities (`ArmPX/NX/PZ/NZ`, `Enabled false` in the .pre, cached on `Ref::arms` at spawn, toggled by `updateArms` toward same-medium neighbours/crossings/capacity-holding buildings); the border wall renders a LOW CubeHalf but keeps a tall 10x20x10 collider (deliberate mismatch — invisible fence above the visible wall); `structureSpawnHeights` = each prefab's box HALF height so everything sits flush) — `cellsFree` refuses overlaps at aim (red ghost) AND at placeStructure (the MP seam), and FREE resource nodes reserve their extractor's future footprint (buildings can't block extractor placement; extracted nodes rely on the standing extractor's own cells). `StructureSystem::actorInFootprint` (a spatial query for GameUnitComponents — units AND player capsules) refuses a placement someone is STANDING in, checked at both seams as well; it is deliberately NOT folded into cellsFree, which doubles as the unit-spawn-point probe and must not refuse a cell just because units stand near it. The GHOST is the real box: `drawStructureGhost` wireframes the exact footprint square × the prefab's height (`spawnHeightOf` = box half height) plus the interior cell lines, green when clear and red when refused — every whitebox building IS a box, so the preview is exact, and the Wall line and Lance anchor draw the same ghost per segment. The placeables sit behind an RTS GRID HOTBAR — 12 slots on the GRID HOTKEYS QWER/ASDF/ZXCV (`c_gridKeys`, always visible in game mode, every slot also CLICKABLE — `slotAtScreenPos` → `activateSlot`, the ONE entry point for key and click; clicks over the hotbar never reach the world): every slot caption is a 3-5 char SHORTHAND from `c_structureShortNames` (indexed by EStructureType) — the SAME table the world tag over a building uses, so a slot and the thing it builds read identically. * UNIT CAPSULES HAVE `Friction 0` (every enemy*.pre; the player keeps 0.3): the steering SETS the body velocity each tick and the SIM LOD ticks tier-1/2 units only every 0.25 / 1 s, so ground friction (unit 0.3 x ground 0.6, ~4 m/s² of deceleration) bled the commanded speed away between ticks — tier-2 units stopped dead inside their interval. Velocity-driven bodies need no friction; do not put it back. The flip side: STOPPING is an explicit command too — `GameUnitComponent::update` BRAKES (planar velocity to zero at the steering accel) whenever it has no walk target or has arrived; without it a coasting unit never stopped, kept splatting its velocity into the crowd lane, and the pack followed the ghost trail (no lane splat while braking)
